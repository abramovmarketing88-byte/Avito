# Stage 3–4: Ad Writing

Use on pipeline stages 3 (base ads) and 4 (post-audit edits).

## When to read

- Stage 3: create 3–4 base ads per approved angle (pain, benefit, urgency, trust).
- Stage 4: fix copy after audit while keeping structure.

## Input from session state

- Selected audience (segment, pains, motivation, objections)
- Approved headlines and special offers
- Selected creative angles
- City/region (if localization needed)
- Brand constraints

## Output format per ad

```markdown
### Ad-{A|B|C|D}: [angle]

**Title:** [headline ~50 chars with keyword]

**Description (HTML):**
[full text with <br>, <p>, lists — as in CSV]

**Keywords:** ...
**Angle:** pain | benefit | urgency | trust
```

## HTML for Avito

- Paragraphs: `<br><br>` or `<p>...</p>`
- Lists: `— item<br>` or `<ul><li>...</li></ul>`
- Emphasis: `<strong>...</strong>`
- Emojis sparingly
- End with CTA and «Добавьте в избранное ❤️»

## Quantity

Create **3–4** base ads — one per approved creative angle.

# Ad Writing Rules

## 1. Start with the client, not the copy

Before writing, understand:

* Who the client is
* What problem they want to solve
* What matters most when choosing
* What objections may arise
* Why they should choose you

Five questions before writing:

1. Main advantage of product/service?
2. Bonus for fast decision?
3. Who uses it and for what?
4. Guarantees and proof of quality?
5. What else should the client know?

---

## 2. Headline is the most important element

A good headline must:

* contain the main search query;
* explain the benefit immediately;
* be clear and interesting.

Example:

❌ Помогу вашему бизнесу

✅ Бухгалтер для ИП и ООО — отчеты без штрафов

Requirements:

* use keywords;
* keep it concise;
* no clickbait that mismatches the body.

---

## 3. Offer must close the main pain

After the headline — offer (~200–300 chars):

* client pain;
* solution;
* USP;
* time limit (if any);
* CTA.

---

## 4. Show who it is for

Short bullets after the offer:

✔ Для ИП  
✔ Для ООО  
✔ Для маркетплейсов  

---

## 5. Why choose you

List advantages, not just services:

* experience;
* guarantee;
* speed;
* cases;
* certificates;
* contract work;
* free consultation.

---

## 6. Strong CTA

* Напишите сейчас.
* Позвоните сегодня.
* Получите бесплатную консультацию.
* Добавьте объявление в избранное.

---

## 7. SEO

Keywords in:

* headline;
* first paragraph;
* end of ad.

Natural placement, no keyword stuffing.

---

## 8. Structured text

* short paragraphs;
* lists;
* spacing between blocks;
* minimal filler.

---

## 9. Photos as a sales tool

First photo drives opens. For services — specialist photo works well.

---

## 10. Price list

Fill if category allows — 5–15 main positions.

---

## 11. Behavioral factors

Track: impressions, CTR, leads, favorites, response speed, rating.

---

## 12. Analyze results

Low leads → fix offer, guarantees, CTA.  
Low impressions → fix headline, keywords, promotion.

---

## 13. Study competitors

Find top ads in category, then create unique positioning — do not copy.

---

# Final ad formula

1. **Headline** with keyword.
2. **Offer**: pain → solution → USP → urgency → CTA.
3. **Who it is for**.
4. **Guarantees and advantages**.
5. **CTA**.
6. **SEO block** with keywords.

## Writing workflow

1. Headline.
2. Selling offer.
3. Target audience fit.
4. Guarantees and advantages.
5. CTA.
6. SEO keywords block.
